Sheet 00 — Design direction

Precision as the brand.

Cosmox sells purity, tolerance and repeatability. The site should behave the same way: a strict grid, hairline rules, oversized type, near-zero decoration, and one accent used only where it means something. Everything below is a token, not a suggestion — it maps directly to tailwind.config.ts in Sheet 10.

A — Palette

Neutrals carry a faint cool-green bias so the paper reads as drafting vellum rather than office white. Teal is the only accent and appears on roughly 2% of any given screen: active nav, links, keyed numerals, one hairline on hover. Rust is semantic only — it flags regulatory notes and export restrictions, never decoration.

The existing teal #16a394 is desaturated and deepened to #0E7C6B so it survives on a light ground without glowing. The corporate navy #073b57 is retired — navy plus teal plus white is the generic chemicals-industry palette, and dropping it is most of what separates this from a template.

B — Typography

Two families, three roles. A neo-grotesque does display and body — single-family typesetting is the Swiss discipline, and the contrast comes from scale and weight rather than a second voice. Monospace is a co-lead, not a caption face: it carries every label, CAS number, specification value and sheet code on the site.

Display — 700, tracking −0.035em, clamp(2.6rem, 6vw, 4.6rem)

everything else.

Body — 400, 16px / 1.62, max 68ch

Every product ships with a technical data sheet, a safety data sheet and a certificate of analysis against the batch. Where a grade is developed to a customer specification, the specification itself becomes the acceptance criterion.

Utility / data — mono, 10.5px, tracking 0.13em, uppercase
CAS 000-00-0Purity ≥ 99.5%Lot 0000-00REACH registered
Font loading — decide before build

The wireframes render in Helvetica Neue with an Arial fallback. For production, licence a real grotesque with a wide weight range and true tabular figures — Söhne, Neue Haas Grotesk Display, ABC Diatype or Aeonik all fit. Self-host as woff2 via next/font/local so there is no CDN dependency and no layout shift. Budget for this: a licensed face is the single biggest visual difference between premium and template.

D — Motion

Restraint is the point. Four movements, one easing curve [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1], everything gated behind prefers-reduced-motion.

MovementWhereSpec
Rule drawSection headingsHairline scales from scaleX(0) to 1 on enter, 600ms. The only decorative motion allowed.

Explicitly rejected

Parallax hero, count-up statistics, glassmorphic cards, gradient text, scroll-jacking, animated background particles, and the five-slide crossfading hero currently on index.html. Each of them is a signal of a template. A single still photograph held at rest, at scale, outperforms all of them on this brief.

Why Services is new — and why it matters most

You asked for a product and service site. Right now the service offering is invisible: custom synthesis, lab-to-commercial scale-up, tailored formulation, regulatory documentation and export handling are all buried as bullet points inside "Capabilities". Those are the highest-margin things Cosmox sells and the reason a buyer picks you over a distributor. Giving them a top-level home with their own enquiry route is the single highest-value change in this plan.

  5. 4Divisions table

    The signature module. Ten divisions as table rows, not a grid of ten cards — a grid of ten cards is what every chemicals competitor ships.   6. 5Capability strip

  7. 6Industries rail

    Horizontally scrolling rail of ten 4:5 cards, last card deliberately cropped by the viewport edge so the overflow is legible. Drag, wheel and keyboard all work; a mono counter reads 01/10. No arrow buttons — they clutter the composition.

  8. 7Numbers

    Full-bleed inverted band, the only ink-ground section on the page. Four figures at 56px in tabular mono with hairline dividers: divisions, countries served, products, years. Numbers are printed, not counted up.

  9. 8Compliance

  10. 9R&D teaser

    Deliberately asymmetric: image occupies columns 1–6, text sits in columns 7–11 and starts 44px lower than the image top. The broken baseline is what keeps a strict grid from reading as rigid. Routes to /services/research.

    One hairline rectangle spanning the full grid. Statement left, two buttons right — "Request a quote" is solid, "Talk to a chemist" is outlined. Two verbs, two different intents, both real.

Assets required before this page can be built

One portrait plant photograph for the hero, ten 4:5 industry images, ten division thumbnails, one lab image, and five certification marks. Photography is the gap the current site's README already flags — the layout is built around a still image doing the work that five animated SVG slides are doing today, so commissioning a half-day plant and lab shoot is a hard dependency, not a nice-to-have.

The one rule that keeps it coherent

Three container treatments exist and no others: bare paper (default), 1px hairline frame (blocks, cards, inputs, images), and inverted ink (stat bands, division CTA). No shadows, no fills, no rounded corners, no gradients. Any new component must land in one of those three or it does not ship. Most premium sites are not doing something clever — they are refusing to do six things at once.

Why JSON and not a CMS

At 10 divisions and 40 products with infrequent updates, a headless CMS adds a subscription, an API dependency and an editor's learning curve for no gain. Two typed JSON files in the repo give full type safety, instant builds and version history. Revisit only if a non-technical editor needs to publish weekly — at which point Sanity drops in behind the same TypeScript types without touching a single component.

C — Critical path
Four things gate launch and none of them are code

1 — Photography. Half a day at the plant and lab. The whole design rests on a small number of good still images. 2 — Product data. CAS numbers, specifications and test methods for the launch set, from the technical team. 3 — Services copy. Four pages that do not exist in any current source. 4 — Contact details. Real department emails and phone numbers. Start all four now; the code cannot wait for them, but the launch will.
